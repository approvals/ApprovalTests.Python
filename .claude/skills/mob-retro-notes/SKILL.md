# Mob retro notes

1. Use the headings from `internal_documentation/peddles_retro.txt`.
   Show all of them from the start, with `…` under empty ones, so people see where things can go.
1. Write lists, not tables. Line breaks inside tables often don't render.
1. Each comment goes under the heading the speaker names, or the one you infer.
   - Start with the speaker's name in bold: `- **Dana:** ...`
   - Keep their own words for the memorable lines, in quotes. Summarize the rest briefly.
   - Put a follow-up or reply as an indented item under the comment it answers.
1. Speaker names are often unreliable:
   - Call audio may arrive as "Speaker 1", "Speaker 2", and the labels can **swap mid-session**.
   - Use clues (names said aloud, who is thanked, who said what earlier) to guess, and mark guesses: `**Franz** *(I think)*`.
   - When a clue later shows a guess was wrong, fix the earlier items or add a visible note asking for corrections.
1. Get people's words right. Don't improve a joke or a quote; if unsure, paraphrase instead of quoting.
1. Use they/them for anyone whose pronouns you haven't heard.
1. Your own suggestions are welcome, but label them as yours (`- *Claude's suggestion:* ...`), and keep data points neutral.
1. When structure changes (e.g. table to lists), check afterwards that no section went missing.
1. At the end, offer to copy the notes into the repo, e.g. a file next to `internal_documentation/peddles_retro.txt`. Humans commit.
